# A criminal label the facts didn't support

*Real case: Young v. CNN, jury verdict January 17, 2025.*

**Aired:** a chyron reading "AFGHANS TRYING TO FLEE TALIBAN FACE BLACK MARKETS, EXORBITANT FEES," in a segment about Navy veteran Zachary Young's evacuation business.

**What was true:** Young ran evacuations paid for by corporate and NGO sponsors, not by desperate individuals. CNN used his LinkedIn solicitation as evidence of a "black market" while leaving out who paid.

**Outcome:** a Florida jury found Young was not a public figure and awarded $5M in compensatory damages; CNN settled before punitive damages. Internal messages showed editors had called the story "a mess."

**What the skeptic should have blocked:** the phrase "black market," which asserts illegality the reporting never established. The fix was either the record showing who paid, or dropping the label.
