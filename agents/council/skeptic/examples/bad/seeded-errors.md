# Seeded errors

*Constructed on purpose.* Plant one of these into a known-good draft and check the skeptic sends it back for that reason. Each maps to a real failure.

| # | Plant this | Real failure it mirrors |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make the agency the subject: "the district attorney was charged with murder" | Hoodline, 2024 |
| 2 | Add a quote that isn't in any source | Cody Enterprise, 2024; Ars Technica, 2026 |
| 3 | Turn a complaint into a fact: "the steering fails" instead of "owners reported the steering failed" | Complaint read as established defect |
| 4 | Add "currently" to a count that runs through an older cutoff | Stale data stated as current |
| 5 | Call it "fraud" when only complaints are cited | Young v. CNN ("black market"), 2025 |

| 8 | Write "no records exist" | MuckRock's no-records causes |
| 9 | Put an unverified causal claim in the headline and the attribution in paragraph 6 | NYT al-Ahli headline, 2023 |
| 10 | Treat a contract ceiling as money spent | DOGE receipts, 2025 |
| 11 | Name a private individual as the subject | Standing rule |
| 12 | Rest a named person's alleged conduct on one anonymous source | Spears v. NYT, 2026 ($9.25M) |
