# Reworking someone else's reporting

*Real case: The Dissent, San Francisco, 2026.*

**What happened:** an AI-run SF news site, seven named AI "reporters," 30–40 articles a day. Mission Local documented stories that closely mirrored its own original investigations, sometimes crediting them and sometimes not linking back.

**Why it's "not new":** the finding already existed in someone else's reporting. Restating it more quickly adds nothing a reader couldn't get from the original.

**What good would look like:** find what the original story didn't establish, pull that record, and lead with it — crediting the original by name.

Source: [Mission Local](https://missionlocal.org/2026/08/the-dissent-uses-ai-reporters-to-cover-s-f-taking-the-work-of-human-journalists/)
