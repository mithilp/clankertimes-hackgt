# A connection nobody had published

*Real case: ProPublica + Medill, "Rx Roulette," December 2025.*

**What was new:** more than 39,000 generic drug products traced to the specific factories that made them — a link the FDA had never published. The key piece was a partial list of manufacturer addresses keyed to drug application numbers, which ProPublica obtained by suing the FDA. They then joined it to inspection results, warning letters, and 15 years of import alerts reconstructed from the Wayback Machine because the FDA doesn't keep lifted ones.

**Why it counts:** a new record (the address list), a new connection (product to factory to inspection history), and a searchable tool nobody else had. It won the 2025 IRE FOI Award and was a 2026 Pulitzer finalist.

Source: [methodology](https://www.propublica.org/article/rx-inspector-fda-generic-drug-tool-methodology)
