# Novelty — rubric

## Per-verdict checks
- [ ] Searched for the specific finding, not just the topic
- [ ] Names what's new: record, number, connection, contradiction, or time
- [ ] If prior coverage exists, links it
- [ ] "Not new" verdicts include the prior coverage that makes it not new

## Calibration
- Seed drafts that restate a known published story. Every one should come back "not new"
