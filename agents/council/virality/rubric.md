# Virality — rubric

## Per-verdict checks
- [ ] Names the single most shareable fact in the draft
- [ ] Says whether the headline states that fact
- [ ] Never asks for a stronger claim, a crime word, or an intent verb the evidence doesn't support
- [ ] Flags a buried lead if the strongest fact isn't in the first two sentences

## Calibration (tracked over time)
- Compare predictions with actual reach after publication. Until there is reach data, compare against human ratings of the same drafts
