# Punching it up past the evidence

*Constructed example of a known failure mode.*

**Draft headline:** "Tesla's Deadly Steering Scandal"

**What the evidence supported:** 15 owner complaints, three recalls, and an open NHTSA engineering analysis — and Tesla's recall filing said it was not aware of crashes, injuries or deaths from the power-steering condition.

**Why it's bad:** "deadly" asserts deaths the records don't show, and "scandal" asserts wrongdoing no record establishes. It would travel further and fail the skeptic, and correctly so.

**What good would look like:** "15 Tesla Model 3 owners reported steering failures; NHTSA has now received 115 reports in a widened investigation." The number and the agency action are what's shareable, and they're true.
