# A number and an official action in the headline

*Real case: Connecticut Mirror + ProPublica, "On the Hook," 2025 (2026 Pulitzer, Local Reporting).*

**Finding:** about 60% of tow companies' requests to sell a car valued it under $1,500 — the threshold that lets them sell it after 15 days instead of 45.

**Why it travels:** a specific number, a specific dollar threshold written into the rules, and stakes anyone who has had a car towed understands. The rule itself makes the argument; the reader doesn't have to take the reporters' word for anything.

Source: [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/article/connecticut-towing-law-data-dmv)
