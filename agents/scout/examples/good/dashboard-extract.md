# The raw data behind a public dashboard

*Real case: The Marshall Project, August 2025.*

**Sub-hypothesis:** DOJ's deaths-in-custody data is incomplete and fails the agency's own reporting standard.

**What the scout-equivalent did:** opened the public Bureau of Justice Assistance dashboard, found the source-data view behind its "Grand Total" row, and exported all 25,393 individual records with 39 fields. No records request. The agency reconfigured the page afterward.

**Then:** compared the records against BJA's own written reporting guide (the yardstick), and against independently compiled state death lists (the falsification set). Found at least 681 known deaths missing, and 77% of a random sample failing the agency's own standard.

**Why it's good:** official records first, the agency's own rule as the test, and an independent second dataset to try to disprove it. Disagreements between the two human coders were resolved in the government's favor.
