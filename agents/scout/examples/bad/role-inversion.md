# The office became the accused

*Real case: Hoodline, October 8, 2024.*

**Published:** "San Mateo County DA Charged With Murder Amid Ongoing Search for Victim's Remains."

**What was true:** the DA's office charged a 56-year-old man with murder. The DA was never accused of anything.

**Mechanism:** the generator scraped the DA's post on X. In plain text, the account's display name ran into the first words of the post, so the office read as the subject of "charged with murder." The correction called it a "typo."

**What would have caught it:** the court docket, or the DA's own press release, both naming the defendant. Or a one-line rule: an office is the charging party in its own releases, never the accused.
