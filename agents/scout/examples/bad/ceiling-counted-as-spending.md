# A contract ceiling counted as money spent, three times

*Real case: DOGE's "wall of receipts," February 2025.*

**Claimed:** billions in savings, including $8 billion on one ICE contract.

**What was true:** that contract was $8 million, mis-entered in the federal record; fixing that one row halved the announced total. Separately, the same $655 million USAID contract ceiling was counted three times — about $1.96 billion claimed against a $655M ceiling and roughly $400M actually spent.

**Rule:** a ceiling is not an obligation, and an obligation is not an outlay. Reconcile all three in USAspending/FPDS, and de-duplicate task orders under their parent contract.
