# Old news, no new trigger

*Constructed example, from the team whiteboard.*

**What Bossman added:** Paul George's 2017 leg injury, because a sports forum thread mentioned it today.

**Why it's bad:** It isn't moving now. A thread mentioning an old event is not a spike, and there's no new accountable action or record. Nothing about it is checkable that wasn't checked in 2017.

**What good would look like:** skip it — unless something new attached to it (a new lawsuit, a new ruling, a new report), in which case the new thing is the item.
