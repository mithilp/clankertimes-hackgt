# Recording a name before the authorities release one

*Real cases.*

**Butler, PA, July 13, 2024.** X accounts, including one with about 1.3M followers, named an Italian soccer YouTuber as the Trump rally shooter, traced back to a joke post. The FBI named the actual shooter the next day. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/italian-sports-journalist-is-falsely-identified-trump-shooter-social-media-2024-07-14/))

**Southport, UK, July 29, 2024.** A pseudo-news site published a false name for the teenage suspect and suggested he was a recent asylum seeker. Posts claiming the attacker was a Muslim migrant drew at least 27 million impressions. He was born in Cardiff. ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y38gjp4ygo))

**Rule:** record the event; never record an identity the investigating agency hasn't released. A name that circulates first is by definition unverified.
