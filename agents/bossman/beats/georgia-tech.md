# Beat: Georgia Tech

## Focus

Georgia Tech as an institution: the Institute's administration, its money, its research enterprise (including GTRI, one of the country's larger university defense contractors), its athletics program, its police department, its housing, and the state and federal bodies that fund and govern it. We are watching for decisions made by people with power over Georgia Tech, and for the records those decisions leave behind.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

The playbook's four tests still apply: moving now, someone accountable, a checkable claim underneath, a records trail nobody has pulled. On this beat they tend to look like:

- **Money.** Tuition and mandatory fee changes, athletics budgets and deficits, NIL deals and the collective behind them, big construction contracts, what the Georgia Tech Foundation and the Georgia Tech Athletic Association report on their tax filings, state appropriations.
- **Federal research money and research security.** Grants cut, frozen or won; the indirect-cost fight; export-control and foreign-influence cases; False Claims Act cases; DoD work at GTRI.
- **Campus safety.** Incidents the Georgia Tech Police Department handled, and whether the daily crime log and the annual Clery report tell the same story.
- **Housing and cost of attending.** Housing shortages, lottery or policy changes, dining contracts, what students are charged and who gets the contract.
- **Governance.** University System of Georgia Board of Regents decisions that land on Georgia Tech, state laws the Institute has to comply with, policy changes on speech, admissions or programs.
- **Labor.** Graduate stipends, TA and RA pay, staff cuts, contractors on campus.

A student complaint thread on r/gatech is a lead when many unrelated posters describe the same institutional decision (a fee, a housing rule, a closure). It is never the story by itself.

**Board of Regents items** count only when they name Georgia Tech or apply to the whole system. A degree approved at Augusta or Savannah State is off the beat.

**Athletics money** is on the beat, but a salary ranking or an NIL think-piece is not a signal by itself. Keep it only when it points at a Georgia Tech Athletic Association document: its Form 990, the NCAA financial report, or the institutional subsidy line in the USG budget.

## What to skip

- Game results, recruiting, rankings, and "Georgia Tech ranked #N" press releases.
- Research press releases ("researchers develop a new material") unless money, safety or a dispute is attached.
- Club events, memes, course or professor complaints, admissions chances threads, individual student drama.
- Conference-wide rankings or trend pieces that only "implicitly" involve Georgia Tech.
- Anything where the subject would be a student. Students are private individuals. Faculty and staff are private unless they are acting in an official role (a dean announcing a policy, a principal investigator on a federal award).

## Where the records usually are

- USG Board of Regents meeting agendas and minutes
- Open Georgia (state salaries and spending) and Georgia Open Records Act requests
- IRS Form 990s for the Georgia Tech Foundation and the Georgia Tech Athletic Association (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer)
- USAspending.gov and NSF/NIH award search for federal grants and contracts to Georgia Tech and GTRI
- NCAA financial reports for athletics revenue and expenses
- GTPD's daily crime log and annual security (Clery) report
- City of Atlanta Legistar and Fulton County property and court records for land and construction around campus
- Federal court dockets for lawsuits naming the Institute

## Searching this beat

Lessons from earlier passes:

- **Never search the bare string "GTRI".** It collides with India's Global Trade Research Initiative. Use "Georgia Tech Research Institute", or "Georgia Tech Research Corporation", the entity name on federal contracts, patents and ITC filings.
- **Keep sports out of news searches** that aren't about athletics money: add `-football -basketball -odds -betting -preview`. Game previews and sportsbook promos otherwise take a quarter of the results.
- **Labor and housing rarely reach news outlets.** News searches for them return almost nothing. Watch r/gatech for many posters describing the same decision, then look for the records.
- **Web searches should be short.** `site:nique.net housing` works; a long list of keywords after `site:` gets ignored and returns pages about the country of Georgia.

## Where to look

Starting points, not limits:

- r/gatech (newest posts) and r/Atlanta
- Google News searches for "Georgia Tech", "Georgia Institute of Technology", "GTRI", "Georgia Tech athletics", and the Board of Regents
- The Technique, the student paper (nique.net; it has no feed, so search it: `site:nique.net` plus a word or two, or a news search for "Technique" and the topic)
- Georgia Tech's own newsroom feed: https://news.gatech.edu/rss/all (what the Institute wants said; useful for what it leaves out)
- The Federal Register, searching for "Georgia Institute of Technology" or "Georgia Tech Research Corporation"
- The Atlanta Journal-Constitution's Georgia Tech coverage, through news search
