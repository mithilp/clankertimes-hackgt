# Beat: Product safety

## Focus

Products that hurt people, and the companies and regulators responsible: recalls, safety investigations, injury and complaint reports, and enforcement actions from the CPSC, FDA, NHTSA, FAA, NTSB and OSHA. The newsroom's first published story came from this kind of record, and it is where records are densest: nearly every event here leaves a filing, a recall notice or a docket behind.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

The playbook's four tests still apply. On this beat they tend to look like:

- **A recall that came late.** A recall notice whose own timeline shows the company knew of injuries or complaints months or years before acting.
- **A gap between what a company tells the public and what it tells a regulator.** Marketing that calls a product safe while incident reports pile up.
- **A pattern in complaints.** Many unrelated owners describing the same failure in NHTSA, FDA MAUDE or CPSC SaferProducts reports, before any recall.
- **Repeat offenders.** A company recalling the same kind of defect again, or an importer whose products keep turning up.
- **Enforcement that stalls.** Investigations open for years, civil penalties negotiated down, fixes that did not fix it.
- **Crashes and accidents with an institutional cause.** NTSB preliminary reports, FAA airworthiness directives, OSHA fatality inspections.

## What to skip

- Routine recalls with no injuries, no pattern and no delay (a mislabeled allergen caught before sale).
- Product reviews, launches and deals.
- Single anecdotes with no record behind them.
- Victims as subjects. Injured people are private individuals; the company and the regulator are the subjects.

## Where the records usually are

- CPSC recall notices, SaferProducts.gov reports, and civil penalty press releases
- FDA recalls, warning letters, MAUDE device reports, FAERS drug reports, CAERS food reports
- NHTSA recalls, defect investigations (ODI) and owner complaints
- FAA airworthiness directives and enforcement; NTSB preliminary and final reports
- OSHA inspection and fatality records
- Federal court dockets for product-liability suits and consolidated litigation

## Searching this beat

- Company plus regulator plus record type: "NHTSA investigation Tesla", "CPSC recall infant", "FDA warning letter".
- The recall notices themselves are the spike; the story is usually in the timeline inside them.

## Where to look

Starting points, not limits:

- CPSC recalls feed: https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/CPSC-RSS-Feed/Recalls-RSS
- FDA recalls feed: https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/contact-fda/stay-informed/rss-feeds/recalls/rss.xml
- The Federal Register for NHTSA, FAA and CPSC notices
- News searches on recalls, investigations and warning letters
- r/cars, r/mildlyinfuriating and product subreddits for owners describing the same failure
