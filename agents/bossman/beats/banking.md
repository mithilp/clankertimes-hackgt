# Beat: Banking and consumer finance

## Focus

Banks, credit unions, fintechs, lenders, payment companies, crypto platforms and the regulators that oversee them: the FDIC, OCC, Federal Reserve, CFPB, NCUA, state banking departments and FinCEN. We are watching for harm to ordinary customers (fees, frozen accounts, lost deposits, debt collection), for regulators easing off or bearing down, and for the plumbing that breaks: bank-fintech partnerships, private credit, buy now pay later, stablecoins.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

- **Enforcement, and its reversal.** Consent orders, civil money penalties and terminations of orders. The CFPB dropping or settling cases is as newsworthy as bringing them: what was the original finding, and what did the company pay or stop paying?
- **Bank failures and troubled banks.** FDIC failures and the cost to the Deposit Insurance Fund, banks under formal agreements, brokered-deposit reliance, commercial real estate losses showing up in call reports.
- **Fintech and banking-as-a-service.** Customers locked out of their money when a middleman fails (the Synapse pattern), sponsor banks under consent orders, fintechs whose deposits aren't insured the way customers think.
- **Consumer harm with numbers.** CFPB complaint database spikes against one company, overdraft and junk fees, debt collectors, credit reporting errors, auto lenders repossessing.
- **Crypto and stablecoins** where a regulator, court or bankruptcy record is involved: exchanges' reserve claims, stablecoin issuers' attestations, state money-transmitter actions.
- **Georgia and Atlanta**: Georgia Department of Banking and Finance actions, Atlanta-based fintechs and payment processors (the city is a payments hub), Fed Atlanta.

## What to skip

- Rate forecasts, mortgage-rate stories, "best savings account" content, market commentary.
- Personal-finance advice threads, individual customer rants with no pattern.
- Crypto price moves and token launches with no regulator, court or filing attached.

## Where the records usually are

- FDIC failed-bank list and BankFind; FDIC, OCC and Fed enforcement action searches
- CFPB enforcement actions (consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions) and the CFPB consumer complaint database
- FinCEN enforcement actions; state banking department orders (Georgia DBF, NYDFS)
- Bankruptcy and federal court dockets (CourtListener); SEC filings of public banks and fintechs (`sec_filings`)
- The Federal Register for agency rules, rescissions and orders

## Where to look

Starting points, not limits:

- Google News for "consent order bank", "CFPB drops case", "bank failure FDIC", "fintech customers frozen", "civil money penalty", "stablecoin attestation"
- `federal_register` for "Consumer Financial Protection Bureau", "Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation"
- `sec_filings` for "consent order | 8-K", "Written Agreement | 10-Q", "brokered deposits | 10-Q"
- Feeds: Federal Reserve enforcement https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/press_enforcement.xml ; OCC news https://www.occ.gov/rss/occ_news.xml
- r/personalfinance and r/banking, only when many unrelated posters describe the same bank or app freezing accounts or changing fees
