# Beat: Public money

## Focus

Where taxpayers' money goes after it is raised: municipal bonds, tax allocation districts and other tax breaks, pension funds, stadium and development deals, and big public contracts. Atlanta, Fulton and DeKalb counties, Atlanta Public Schools and the state of Georgia first; other large cities and states when the record is unusually good. The beat's primary records are bond disclosures on MSRB's EMMA, audits, budgets, pension board minutes and procurement records.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

- **Subsidies against promises.** A tax break or tax allocation district justified by jobs, housing or schools money, and what the later audit, bond disclosure or annual report shows actually happened.
- **Bond disclosures that contradict the politics.** Continuing disclosures on EMMA that report a revenue shortfall, a covenant problem, a draw on reserves or a late filing while officials say all is well.
- **Pensions.** Funding ratios, assumed returns, fees paid to private equity and hedge funds, board votes that shift risk.
- **Contracts.** No-bid and emergency contracts, change orders that multiply a contract's price, the same vendor winning repeatedly, contractors who are also campaign donors.
- **Stadiums, arenas and "megaprojects"** (the Beltline, airport concessions, data centers seeking tax abatements): the public cost per job, and who holds the risk.
- **Legal but surprising** counts: an arrangement that is entirely lawful but that residents would not expect, told with the exact numbers from the record.

## What to skip

- Budget-season horse race coverage and campaign rhetoric with no document behind it.
- Generic "national debt" and federal deficit stories.
- Routine bond sales by large issuers with nothing unusual in the disclosure.

## Where the records usually are

- MSRB EMMA (emma.msrb.org): official statements and continuing disclosures; bond documents restate tax digests and revenue by district
- City auditors (atlaudit.org for Atlanta), the Georgia Department of Audits and Accounts, state comptroller reports
- Invest Atlanta and Development Authority of Fulton County board agendas and abatement lists
- Atlanta City Council and county commission agendas (Legistar), Open Georgia (salaries and state spending)
- Pension board minutes and annual reports (Employees' Retirement System of Georgia, Teachers Retirement System, City of Atlanta pension funds)
- USAspending.gov for federal money flowing to local projects

## Where to look

Starting points, not limits:

- Google News for "tax allocation district", "tax abatement Atlanta", "Development Authority of Fulton County", "pension fund private equity fees", "no-bid contract", "change order", "stadium bonds"
- Rough Draft Atlanta, Atlanta Civic Circle, the AJC and Capital B Atlanta through news search
- `web_search` with site:atlaudit.org, site:audits.ga.gov, site:emma.msrb.org
- `federal_register` only for federal grants and programs that fund local projects
- r/Atlanta when many posters point at the same public project or contract
