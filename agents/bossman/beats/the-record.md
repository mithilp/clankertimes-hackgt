# Beat: The record

## Focus

What actually happened, now that the records are in. Big government programs, promises and decisions from past administrations (Obama, Trump's first term, Biden) and from recent years in Georgia, measured against the audits, inspector general reports, spending data, court records and filings that came out after the headlines moved on. The story is the gap between what was promised or claimed at the time and what the record shows now, told with a number nobody has computed.

This beat is not about current events. A 2009, 2017 or 2021 program is fair game when a new record (an audit, an IG report, a data release, a court ruling, a settlement) lets us say something new about it.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

- **Promise against result.** Jobs promised for a tax break, loan program or grant, against jobs reported; money announced against money actually spent; projects promised against projects finished.
- **Where the money went.** Pandemic relief (PPP, EIDL, CARES Act, ARPA state and local funds), the 2009 Recovery Act, Opportunity Zones (2017), the Inflation Reduction Act and CHIPS Act grants, broadband money, disaster aid: who got it, how much went to places or companies that didn't fit the program's purpose, how much was never spent or clawed back.
- **Fraud and waste the records quantify.** Inspector general and GAO findings, DOJ settlements and False Claims Act cases, with totals nobody has added up for one state, industry or company.
- **Georgia and Atlanta first:** how federal programs landed here (Opportunity Zones in Atlanta, PPP loans to Georgia companies, ARPA money Georgia hasn't spent, the Beltline, MARTA expansion promises, Hyundai and Rivian incentives).
- **An old claim that a new record settles.** Officials said X at the time; a later audit, court ruling or data release shows Y.

## What to skip

- Opinion pieces and partisan retrospectives with no record behind them.
- Programs whose results are already thoroughly covered, unless a new record changes the numbers.
- Anything that needs a records request to settle.

## Where the records usually are

- GAO reports (gao.gov) and agency inspector general reports (oversight.gov searches all IG reports at once)
- USAspending.gov and the Pandemic Response Accountability Committee's data (pandemicoversight.gov)
- SBA's public PPP and EIDL loan data
- Treasury's ARPA State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds reports
- Department of Justice press releases and False Claims Act settlements; federal court dockets (CourtListener)
- The Federal Register for program rules and rescissions
- Georgia Department of Audits and Accounts, Georgia Department of Economic Development incentive reports, Invest Atlanta and development authority records

## Where to look

Starting points, not limits:

- Google News for "inspector general report", "GAO found", "False Claims Act settlement", "clawback", "never spent", "jobs promised" plus a program name (PPP, ARPA, Opportunity Zone, CHIPS, Inflation Reduction Act, broadband, Recovery Act)
- `federal_register` for program names
- `web_search` with site:oversight.gov, site:gao.gov, site:pandemicoversight.gov, site:justice.gov
- Feeds: GAO reports https://www.gao.gov/rss/reports.xml ; Oversight.gov https://www.oversight.gov/rss.xml
