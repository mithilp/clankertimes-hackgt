# Beat: Higher education

## Focus

American colleges and universities as institutions with money and power: what students pay, where tuition and federal money go, how schools treat their workers and students, and how federal and state governments are reshaping them. Georgia Tech has its own beat; this one covers the rest of the country, and the national decisions that land on every campus.

## What "interesting" means on this beat

The playbook's four tests still apply. On this beat they tend to look like:

- **Federal money.** Research grants frozen or terminated, the indirect-cost fight, investigations and settlements with the federal government, student-aid changes.
- **What students pay.** Tuition and fee increases, housing costs, financial-aid policy changes, and schools' own net-price data.
- **Closures and cuts.** Colleges closing or merging, programs eliminated, faculty and staff layoffs, and what the budgets show.
- **Athletics money.** Revenue sharing with athletes, NIL collectives, athletic deficits covered by student fees.
- **Campus safety and Title IX.** Clery reports, federal findings, settlements.
- **Governance.** Boards and legislatures overruling faculty, presidents' contracts, state laws on what can be taught.
- **Accreditation and for-profit colleges.** Accreditor actions, federal findings of misrepresentation, borrower-defense discharges.

## What to skip

- Rankings, admissions tips, commencement news and research press releases with nothing at stake.
- Individual students as subjects. Students are private individuals.
- Culture-war commentary with no action or record behind it.

## Where the records usually are

- USAspending.gov and NSF/NIH award search for federal research money
- IPEDS and College Scorecard for tuition, net price, enrollment and outcomes
- IRS Form 990s for university foundations and athletic associations; NCAA financial reports
- Department of Education findings, Title IX and civil-rights resolutions, and Clery reports
- State budgets, board of regents and trustee minutes
- Federal court dockets for suits by and against universities

## Searching this beat

- A school or system plus the action: "university layoffs budget", "grant terminated NIH university", "college closure", "tuition increase board of regents".
