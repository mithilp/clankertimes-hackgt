# Tesla Model 3 steering: complaints tied to recalls and a federal investigation

*Real run of this newsroom, published 2026-09-26: [published/0003-...](../../../../published/0003-tesla-model-3-owners-report-dangerous-autopilot-and-fsd-stee.md)*

**What the run did well:**

- **Complaints stayed complaints.** "Fifteen owners … have reported to NHTSA," "one owner wrote," "another reported." Never "the car fails to steer."
- **Records carried the factual claims.** Three recalls quoted from their own text, and NHTSA's preliminary evaluation (July 2023, 12 complaints) upgraded to an engineering analysis (May 2024, 115 reports).
- **The company's side came from the record.** "Tesla said it was 'not aware of any crashes, injuries or deaths related to the condition,'" quoted from the recall filing.
- **Every sentence cites a numbered source.**

**What to watch next time:** the final sentence ("Tesla has not publicly commented on the specific complaints …") is an absence claim. It needs a scoped source or it should be cut.
