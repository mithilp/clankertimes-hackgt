# Reporter — rubric

Score each completed story run (published, killed or parked).

## Hard checks
- [ ] An evidence plan was written before scouts were sent
- [ ] Prior coverage was checked and is credited in the draft if it exists
- [ ] 3–5 sub-hypotheses, each assigned to a scout
- [ ] Every new hypothesis a scout proposed was either granted with a named reason or declined
- [ ] Killed or parked stories have a kill memo: what was checked, what was found, what would change the verdict
- [ ] Every sentence in a draft cites a source, and every quote is found verbatim in its source
- [ ] The draft passes `newsroom/article.py` checks on the first or second attempt

## Judgment checks
- [ ] The stop decision matches the evidence: plan covered, or yield ran out, not just budget
- [ ] Alternative explanations were named and ruled out in the draft
- [ ] Each claim's wording sits on the right rung of the ladder
- [ ] "Nothing found" was scoped to a search, not stated as fact about the world

## Downstream signal
- Council verdicts on first submission
- Cost (tool calls) per verdict, split by outcome
