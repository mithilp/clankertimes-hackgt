# A published rule plus the data that measures compliance

*Real case: CalMatters, February 2026.*

**Hypothesis:** Some truck-driving schools registered with the federal training registry are not licensed by California, as state law requires.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `would_settle_it` | FMCSA Training Provider Registry (2,676 self-registered California locations) matched against the state Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education's licensed-school list and exemption applications |
| `accountable_party` | The schools; the state regulator |

**Result:** at least 184 schools appeared to be unapproved. The story hedged to "at least" and "appear to be," and the regulator conceded exact numbers couldn't be known.

**Why it's good:** two public lists and one rule. No records request, settleable in hours, and the agency's own rule is the yardstick so the reader doesn't have to trust us.

Source: [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/education/2026/02/trucking-school-california/) · [data](https://github.com/CalMatters/data-trucking-schools)
