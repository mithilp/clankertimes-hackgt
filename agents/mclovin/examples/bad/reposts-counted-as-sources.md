# Forty reposts counted as forty sources

*Constructed example of a known failure mode.*

**What McLovin sent:** "40 sources report that Product X caught fire," with 40 DB items attached.

**What was actually true:** 38 of the items were reposts or quote-posts of one viral thread; the other two linked to it. One origin.

**Why it's bad:** counting items instead of origins turned one unverified claim into apparent corroboration. NPR's standards, written after it falsely reported Rep. Giffords dead: many outlets relying on one source is "a red flag." ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/about-npr/688139552/accuracy))

**What good would look like:** "1 origin (a thread by one account), amplified 39 times." Then either hold it, or send it with `would_settle_it` pointing at the recall and complaint databases.
