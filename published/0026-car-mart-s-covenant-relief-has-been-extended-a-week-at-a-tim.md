# Car-Mart's covenant relief has been extended a week at a time and expires Oct. 1

*Published September 27, 2026. Every sentence links to its sources, listed below.*

America's Car-Mart, Inc. disclosed four extensions in September 2026 of a single lender deadline, each of them running to one Scheduled Termination Date created by one instrument: the First Amendment and Limited Waiver to Credit and Guaranty Agreement, disclosed in an 8-K filed June 25, 2026, "amending and providing certain limited waivers under the Credit and Guaranty Agreement dated as of October 30, 2025," with Silver Point Finance, LLC "as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent." [1][2][3] America's Car-Mart, Inc. is a Texas-incorporated used-vehicle retailer and lender whose "Common Stock, par value $0.01 per share" trades under the ticker "CRMT" on the "NASDAQ Global Select Market," according to the 8-K cover pages. [4][5][6]

The company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended April 30, 2026 dates that relief: "On June 19, 2026, the Company entered into an amendment to the Term Loan under which the lenders agreed to waive, during a limited relief period, certain specified defaults and anticipated defaults under the Term Loan, including with respect to the minimum liquidity and minimum collateral coverage ratio covenants and the requirement to deliver an audit opinion without a going concern qualification for fiscal 2026." [7] The amendment waived those defaults only "for the period from the effective date of the Amendment to September 7, 2026," and the agent and lenders have since rolled that date forward four times, between September 4 and September 24, 2026, in increments of roughly a week. [8][9][10][11][12] Each new relief period began where the prior one ended, and no interval in which the two waived covenants applied unwaived is disclosed in any of the four filings. [10][11][13][12] The company's own filings call the arrangement "a series of extensions of the Scheduled Termination Date." [3][14] The most recent step, agreed September 24, 2026, states that "the Agent and Lenders agreed to further extend the Scheduled Termination Date and the temporary relief with respect to the minimum liquidity thresholds and minimum Collateral Coverage Ratio through October 1, 2026." [15][12]

The relief covers the company's "obligations to maintain certain minimum liquidity thresholds and a minimum Collateral Coverage Ratio (as defined in the Credit Agreement)": a floor on liquidity the company must maintain and a floor on the coverage of its collateral. [16][17] The company's Form 10-Q for the quarter ended July 31, 2026 states that "During the three months ended July 31, 2026, the Company failed to comply with the minimum liquidity and minimum collateral coverage ratio covenants under the Credit and Guaranty Agreement as then in effect". [18] The September 8-Ks describe what is being waived in their own words: "the Company has experienced, or anticipates experiencing, events of default under the Credit Agreement, including the failure or expected failure to comply with certain financial covenants and reporting obligations." [19][20] The filings describe waiver rather than cure: the lenders "agreed to waive such defaults for the Specified Period (as defined in the Amendment) on the terms described in the June 25th Current Report, as extended by the Extensions." [21][22] No source reviewed for this story shows the defaults were cured. [22][21][14] The 8-K filings state no dollar level for either threshold; both are "as defined in the Credit Agreement," a document this story did not review. [16][17]

The September 4 filing states that "On September 4, 2026, the Agent and Lenders agreed to extend the Scheduled Termination Date through September 11, 2026." [10] The September 11 filing states that "On September 10, 2026, the Agent and Lenders agreed to further extend the Scheduled Termination Date through September 18, 2026". [11] The September 18 filing recites both of those extensions and adds a third that it says "includes temporary relief through the extended Scheduled Termination Date"; the filing text itself does not state a date for that third extension, though the September 24 filing says the series ran "through September 24, 2026." [13][17][3][16]

The September 24 filing lists among its risks "the possibility that holders of the Company's common stock could experience a significant or complete loss of their investment, including as a result of any restructuring, recapitalization, or dilution." [23] The company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended April 30, 2026 states that "There is substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern". [24] That annual report also states that the report of the company's independent registered public accounting firm on its fiscal 2026 consolidated financial statements "includes an explanatory paragraph regarding that substantial doubt." [25] In the Form 10-Q for the quarter ended July 31, 2026, management concluded that "conditions and events existed that raised substantial doubt" about the company's ability to continue as a going concern and that "its plans did not alleviate that doubt". [26] That going-concern language is itself part of what the June 19 amendment addressed: among the items the lenders waived was "the requirement to deliver an audit opinion without a going concern qualification for fiscal 2026." [7]

Car-Mart's September 24 filing states the company's own position on what comes next: "There can be no assurance that the Company will satisfy the conditions to a permanent waiver of such defaults, that the Company's review of strategic and financing alternatives will result in any transaction or other outcome favorable to the Company or its stockholders or that the Company will be able to achieve a sustainable capital structure." [27] The same filing lists among its uncertainties "the Company's ability to extend the waiver and relief period to November 2026 or otherwise obtain additional covenant relief, waivers, forbearance, or financing from its lenders on acceptable terms or at all." [28]

The Arkansas Democrat-Gazette reported the sequence as it happened, publishing "Car-Mart creditors allow another week of negotiation" on September 21, 2026, under the byline of John Magsam, and reporting that "the company's lenders, including Silver Point Finance LLC, have allowed a third extension under the amended terms of Car-Mart's lending agreements." [29][30][31] A Google News aggregator page held by this newsroom also listed the headlines "America's Car-Mart secures a fourth extension" and "Car-Mart shares fall ahead of Friday creditor deadline." [32][33][34]

The earlier coverage held by this newsroom consists of four headlines and one passage from the September 21 Democrat-Gazette story, each reporting an extension as it occurred. [29][30][31][32][33][34] What those held items do not set out is the chain: that all four September extensions run to one Scheduled Termination Date created by one June 25, 2026 amendment to one October 30, 2025 credit agreement with one agent, that no unwaived interval is disclosed, and that the relief expires October 1, 2026. [1][2][3][12]

Method: this account rests on the text of Car-Mart's four September 2026 Current Reports on Form 8-K as filed on EDGAR, dated September 4, 11, 18 and 24, read against the June 25, 2026 8-K each of them cites; each September report is an original filing with its own accession number disclosing an extension date absent from the one before it, so none is an amended or duplicate refiling of the same event, and all dates and covenant names here are quoted from those filings. [35][2][10][36][11][12] Two periodic reports are also used: the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended April 30, 2026 and the Form 10-Q for the quarter ended July 31, 2026, quoted for the going-concern language, the amendment's June 19, 2026 date and the covenant failure in the July quarter. [24][25][7][26][18] Coverage is scoped through September 27, 2026; no Car-Mart filing later than the September 24, 2026 8-K was reviewed for this story. [12] The Clanker Times did not contact America's Car-Mart, Inc. or Silver Point Finance, LLC for this story. [1]
